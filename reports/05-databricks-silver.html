Critical transform: wide → long sales

Bronze keeps competition-native wide columns. Silver unpivots them and maps day_id to real dates via calendar.

Silver (long)

  • ✓0 unmatched calendar days
  • ✓0 null item/store/date keys
  • ✓0 negative quantities
  • ✓0 duplicate item×store×day rows
  • ✓Partitioned by date for prune-friendly reads
